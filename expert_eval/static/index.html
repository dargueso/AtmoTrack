DANA Expert Check

Help us evaluate a cut-off low detector

You will see upper-air maps of the Iberian Peninsula and surroundings. For each map, tell us whether there is a DANA (cut-off low) and where its centre is. Your judgement is the reference: we use it to find where our algorithm needs to improve.

How it works

  1. Each map shows 500 hPa geopotential height (colours, dam), 850 hPa temperature (blue contours, K) and 850 hPa wind (barbs).
  2. Answer: is there a DANA whose centre lies inside the dashed box?
  3. If yes, click the approximate centre of each DANA. You can mark more than one; click a marker to remove it.
  4. Use the time controls to look 24 h before and after, if it helps. Your answer refers to the time marked T+0.
  5. Tick Unsure when the case is genuinely ambiguous. Those answers are very useful too.
  6. You can end the training at any time and see how the algorithm compared with you.
